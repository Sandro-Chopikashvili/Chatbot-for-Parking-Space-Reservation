# Parking Details

- Payment: card or mobile app at the exit kiosk. Cash is not accepted.
- EV charging: 6 chargers in Zone C (Type 2 connector).
- Security: CCTV on all levels and a security guard on site at night.
- Lost ticket: contact the front desk with your car number.
- Maximum vehicle length: 5.2 m. Motorcycles have a separate area in Zone B.
- Pets are allowed in vehicles. Please do not leave animals unattended.
