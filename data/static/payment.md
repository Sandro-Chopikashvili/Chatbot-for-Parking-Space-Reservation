# Payment Methods

Payment is made at the exit kiosk or in the CityPark mobile app. Accepted: debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash is not accepted.

Kiosk: insert your ticket or enter your car number, then pay. After paying, you have 15 minutes to leave the facility.

Mobile app: link a card, then scan the QR code at the entrance or enter your car number. You are charged automatically when you leave.

Receipts: receipts are emailed or printed at the kiosk. For a business receipt with company details, contact the front desk.

Reservations: there is no prepayment. Payment is due at exit, even for reserved spaces.

Refunds: if you were charged incorrectly, contact the front desk within 7 days and bring your receipt.
