# EV Charging

Zone C offers 6 EV chargers with Type 2 connectors. Charging is available only in Zone C and only for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles.

How to charge: park in a marked EV space, connect your own Type 2 cable, then start the session in the CityPark mobile app or with a contactless card at the charger. Stop the session the same way before unplugging.

Cables: the chargers have no attached cables. Bring your own Type 2 cable, because spare cables are not available to borrow.

Etiquette: please move your vehicle within 15 minutes after charging finishes so others can use the charger. Non-electric vehicles must not park in EV spaces.

Reservations: a reservation in Zone C does not guarantee a working charger on arrival, but staff will help if a charger is out of order.

Support: if a charger shows an error, contact the front desk and give the charger number. Charging is billed separately from parking.
