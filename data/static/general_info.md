# About CityPark

CityPark is a covered, 24-hour-monitored parking facility in the city center.
It has 40 spaces across three zones (A, B, C). Zone A is closest to the exit
and best for short stays. Zone B is the standard zone. Zone C has EV charging.
The facility is accessible for wheelchair users, with 4 reserved accessible
spaces in Zone A. Vehicles up to 2.0 m in height are allowed.
