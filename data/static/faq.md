# FAQ

**Can I extend my reservation?** Yes, contact the administrator before it ends.
**What if I arrive late?** The space is held for 30 minutes after the start time.
**Do you offer monthly passes?** Yes, ask the front desk for details.
**Is there a height limit?** Yes, 2.0 m.
**Can I get a receipt?** Yes, receipts are emailed or printed at the kiosk.
