# Accessibility

CityPark is fully accessible for wheelchair users. There are 4 reserved accessible spaces in Zone A, next to the elevator and the exit. These spaces are wider than standard spaces so doors can open fully.

Access: the entrance and exit are step-free. Two elevators connect all levels and have audio announcements and Braille buttons. Ramps have handrails on both sides.

Eligibility: accessible spaces are for vehicles displaying a valid disability parking permit. Please show your permit to the security guard on request.

Assistance: front desk staff can help with luggage, boarding or finding your way. Tell the front desk in advance if you need help on arrival.

Service animals: service animals are welcome throughout the facility.

Reserving: to request an accessible space, contact the administrator when you book.
