# Parking Rules

Please follow these rules to keep CityPark safe for everyone.

Speed limit: 10 km/h inside the facility, on all levels and ramps.
Smoking: smoking and open flames are strictly forbidden anywhere in the building, including inside parked vehicles.
Parking: park only inside the marked lines of your space. Do not block aisles, ramps, fire exits or other vehicles.
Engines: switch off the engine while parked. Idling is allowed only briefly while queuing at the barrier.
Hazardous goods: storing fuel canisters, gas cylinders or other flammable materials in a vehicle is not allowed.
Washing and repairs: washing, repairing or refueling vehicles inside the facility is not permitted. The front desk can arrange emergency roadside help.
Valuables: CityPark is not responsible for items left in vehicles. Lock your car and take valuables with you.
Overstay: vehicles left more than 24 hours past the end of a reservation may be moved at the owner's expense. Contact the front desk if you are delayed.
