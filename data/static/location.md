# Location and Directions

CityPark is at 12 Rustaveli Avenue, Tbilisi, next to the central metro station.
The entrance is on the east side of the building, on Kostava Street.
By metro: exit at Rustaveli station, a 2-minute walk.
By car: enter from Kostava Street and follow the signs for "CityPark".
Nearby landmarks: Opera House (300 m), Freedom Square (700 m).
